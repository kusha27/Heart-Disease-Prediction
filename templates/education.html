Health education

Heart-healthy guidance

The following recommendations are general educational information and should not replace individualized medical advice.

🥗
Heart-healthy eating

Focus on fruits, vegetables, whole grains, beans, lean proteins, and healthy fats while limiting salt, sugar, and ultra-processed foods.

🏃
Regular physical activity

Moderate activity such as brisk walking, cycling, or swimming can help improve blood pressure, cholesterol, and cardiovascular fitness.

🚭
Smoking cessation

Stopping tobacco use can substantially reduce heart disease risk over time and improve circulation and blood vessel health.

🩺
Blood pressure and cholesterol

Keep blood pressure and cholesterol within recommended ranges by monitoring them routinely and following professional guidance.

🩸
Diabetes management

Good glycemic control, medication adherence, and regular check-ins can reduce long-term cardiovascular complications.

📅
Routine medical checkups

Periodic preventive visits help identify risk factors earlier and improve long-term outcomes for heart and vascular health.

⚠️
Symptoms requiring urgent care

Chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden weakness, severe sweating, fainting, or unusual pressure in the chest warrant urgent medical evaluation. If symptoms are severe or are accompanied by fainting, call emergency services immediately.
